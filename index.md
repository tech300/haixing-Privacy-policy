Privacy Policy for 해성 선생님

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

해성 선생님 (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Chinese language conversation practice application designed for Korean-speaking learners. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used, with whom it is shared, and what choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
1. Information We Process

Audio Recordings. When you press and hold the microphone button or use the shadow-reading feature, the App records your voice through the device microphone. The recording is immediately converted to a 16 kHz / 16-bit / mono WAV format and transmitted to our AI service provider for speech recognition. We do not retain the original recording file on your device after the session ends; the temporary .caf file is deleted immediately after conversion.

Transcribed Speech Content. The text produced from your speech (“transcript”) is sent to our AI service provider to generate a tutor response. Transcripts are also used locally on your device to compute pronunciation similarity scores during shadow-reading exercises.

AI-Generated Content. The App generates Chinese tutor responses, Korean translations, and optional grammar corrections. These are displayed to you and spoken aloud through text-to-speech. Generated content is stored only in the App’s short-term conversation memory (limited to the most recent 16 turns) and is not transmitted to any analytics or advertising service.

Subscription and Purchase Information. If you purchase a subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may provide us with a transaction identifier and subscription status so that we can unlock paid features and honor restore requests.

Device and Usage Data. The App does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or advertising identifier. The App does not create a user account.
2. How We Use Information

We process the information described above solely to:

    Convert your speech to text so that the App can understand what you said;

    Generate a contextually appropriate tutor response in Chinese, along with a Korean translation and optional correction;

    Produce spoken audio output of the tutor’s response;

    Compute pronunciation similarity scores when you use the shadow-reading feature;

    Verify subscription status and restore previously purchased subscriptions;

    Enforce the free trial limit described in Section 5.

We do not use your audio, transcripts, or generated content for advertising, profiling, or training our own models.
3. Third-Party AI Service Provider — DeepSeek

To provide speech recognition, conversational AI, and text-to-speech, the App transmits certain data to DeepSeek (operated by Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Basic Technology Research Co., Ltd.), an external AI service provider. This is the disclosure required by App Store Review Guideline 5.1.2(i).

The specific data sent to DeepSeek is:

    Audio data: your recorded WAV file, sent to DeepSeek’s /v1/tools/asr endpoint for transcription;

    Text prompts: your transcript and the recent conversation history (up to 16 turns), sent to DeepSeek’s /v1/chat/completions endpoint to generate the tutor’s response;

    Tutor response text: sent to DeepSeek’s /v1/tools/tts endpoint to produce spoken audio, if the premium text-to-speech voice is used.

DeepSeek processes this data on our behalf to return the requested output. DeepSeek’s privacy practices are governed by its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review. We do not authorize DeepSeek to use your data for its own advertising or model-training purposes beyond what is necessary to provide the service to the App. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Your consent is obtained inside the App before the first AI request is made. A consent screen explains what data is sent to DeepSeek, why, and how to decline. If you decline, AI-powered speech recognition, conversation, and text-to-speech features will not function; you may still use any offline features that do not require transmission.
4. Data Retention

Audio recordings are deleted from your device immediately after WAV conversion. Conversation history is held in memory for the current session and is discarded when you close the App or switch scenarios. Subscription-related records are retained only as long as necessary to verify entitlement and comply with legal obligations. We do not maintain a server-side database of your conversations.
5. Free Trial

New users receive a one-time, cumulative 5-minute free trial of AI-powered conversation features. Trial usage is tracked locally on your device. Once the trial is exhausted, AI conversation features require an active subscription. We do not use the trial to collect additional personal data.
6. Subscriptions and Payment

Subscriptions are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Auto-renewable subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID subscription settings. We do not process refunds directly; refund requests are handled by Apple.
7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.
8. Your Choices and Rights

You may decline AI data-sharing consent, in which case AI features will be unavailable. You may stop using the App at any time. Because we do not maintain user accounts or a server-side conversation database, there is no stored profile for us to delete; however, you may contact us with any privacy request. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to processing. We will respond to verifiable requests within the time required by applicable law.
9. Security

We use HTTPS for all transmissions to DeepSeek. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the limited data the App processes.
10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated through the App or the App Store listing before they take effect.
